# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want CyberNova Lab to be a welcoming learning space, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability. Questions asked in good faith are welcome. Nobody should be mocked for being new.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient, and specific.
- Give constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy, consent, and legal boundaries.
- Credit other people's ideas and contributions.
- Help keep all examples safe and educational.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, or encouraging unauthorised security activity is not accepted.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the repository owner through the contact method listed in their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled respectfully and as confidentially as possible.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
